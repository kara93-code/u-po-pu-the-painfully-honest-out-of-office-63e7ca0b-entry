One sentence, at most 200 characters: a brutally honest but still polite out-of-office auto-reply. Written by `main.ts` to `/output/entry.txt`. The program holds the chosen sentence, checks it is a single sentence of 1–200 characters, then writes it.

Chosen sentence (Version B, 162 characters): "I'm away until Monday, having finally escaped our weekly status meeting to recharge, and I'll reply with a clearer head once I've remembered why I loved this job." The escape from the weekly status meeting is the private truth, framed warmly enough for a manager, landing on a quotable closing line.
